2. What you need to use GasDay

GasDay runs on Android phones. You need a Google account to sign in, an internet connection, and either the free trial or an active GasDay subscription (section 3).

3. Subscription

Free trial. A new GasDay account starts with a free trial, for a number of days or a number of lists made, as shown in the app. The trial is measured with our server's time. During the trial you can use GasDay fully, except saving files to or importing files from your phone's storage. Each phone can get the free trial only once: another account on the same phone does not get a new trial. No payment is taken for the trial.

Subscription. After the free trial, adding or editing customers and making lists need an active subscription. Two plans are offered: monthly and yearly. The exact price of each plan, in your local currency, is shown in the app and in Google Play before you pay. Sometimes an introductory price (for example a lower price for the first month) is offered to new subscribers; it is shown before you pay, and the normal price applies after it.

The subscription is sold and billed by Google Play. By subscribing you also agree to Google Play's terms of service.

4. Billing and renewal

Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you subscribe. The subscription then renews automatically at the end of every month or year, at the price shown when you subscribed (after any introductory price, the normal price), until you cancel it. Taxes are included in the price where the law requires it.

If we change a price, Google Play tells you and, where required, asks for your approval before the new price applies. If a renewal payment fails, Google Play may retry for a short time. During that time GasDay may stay available or may be paused, as Google Play decides.

5. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in Google Play: Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > GasDay.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Uninstalling GasDay or deleting your GasDay account does not cancel the subscription, so please cancel it in Google Play first.

Payments are made to Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. We cannot refund a payment directly. You can ask for a refund in Google Play (Account > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history > the order > Request a refund / Report a problem).

The subscription is linked to the Google account you used to sign in to GasDay. If you reinstall GasDay or use a new phone, sign in with the same Google account and the subscription works again automatically. Signing in with a different Google account does not use this subscription: that account needs a subscription of its own.

A subscription cannot be shared or transferred between Google accounts.

8. If the subscription ends

When the free trial has ended and there is no active subscription, GasDay opens in view-only mode: you can still see your customers and lists, but you cannot add or edit customers or make new lists, and the subscription page is shown. Your data is not deleted: customers and lists stay on your phone in GasDay's storage, exported files stay in Documents/GasDay, and your backup stays in your own Google Drive. When you subscribe with the same Google account, everything is available again. You can log out or delete your account at any time.
