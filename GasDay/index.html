Privacy Policy — GasDay

Last Updated: 10 October 2026

1. Introduction

GasDay is an application designed to help users manage their LPG delivery-related activities and app data. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how GasDay stores, processes, and protects your information.

2. Information We Collect

GasDay processes the information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Account information: Information required to authenticate users and maintain their accounts.
  • App data: Information created or managed within GasDay that is necessary for the app's functionality and backup.

The information processed depends on how you use the application.

3. Google Drive Backup and Data Storage

GasDay uses the user's Google Drive to store app backups and support data restoration.

When you enable the backup feature, GasDay requests the necessary Google Drive permissions to access the storage required for this functionality.

Backup data is stored in the user's own Google Drive account rather than being treated as a backup stored in the developer's personal Google Drive.

Access to Google Drive is subject to the permissions granted by the user and the applicable Google services.

Users are responsible for maintaining access to their Google accounts and ensuring that sufficient storage is available for their backups.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information only as necessary to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain account access.
  • Provide GasDay's functionality.
  • Back up and restore app data using Google Drive.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application.
  • Process account deletion requests.

5. Data Sharing

GasDay does not sell users' personal information.

Information is processed by the services necessary to operate the application, including Google services used for authentication or Google Drive backup, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Account Deletion

GasDay provides an account deletion option on the Delete Account page within the application.

When a user confirms account deletion, the account and its associated data covered by the deletion process are permanently deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Users should understand that data stored separately in their personal Google Drive may require separate deletion, depending on how the backup system is implemented. Account deletion does not automatically guarantee the removal of every backup copy unless the application explicitly performs that operation.

7. Data Security

GasDay uses appropriate security measures to help protect account information and app data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Google Drive access is governed by the permissions granted to the application and Google's applicable security mechanisms.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to GasDay's functionality or applicable legal requirements. The latest revision date will be displayed at the beginning of this policy.
