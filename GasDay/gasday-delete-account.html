Delete your GasDay account

GasDay is an app by Paiotorr. Use this page to request deletion of your account and the data associated with it, without opening the app.

Sign in

Sign in with the same Google account you use in GasDay. This lets us be sure the request comes from the account owner. You will see exactly what will be deleted before anything is requested.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

  • Your GasDay sign-in account (name and email linked to it).
  • Your cloud settings (Shop Name and Location).
  • Your GasDay backup stored in your own Google Drive (the app's private data folder). Google will ask you to allow access to this folder only, so that we can remove it.

What is not deleted by us

  • Data that exists only on your phone (for example the Documents/GasDay folder and PDFs you exported). You can remove it yourself by deleting those files or uninstalling the app.

When it happens

Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion 30 days after your request. During those 30 days you can change your mind: open GasDay, sign in, and choose Recover Account. After 30 days the deletion is final and cannot be undone.

Can't sign in?

Email pulinoffice7@gmail.com from the email address linked to your GasDay account with the subject GasDay account deletion request, and we will process it.
