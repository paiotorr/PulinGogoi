Privacy Policy for Zistcover

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Zistcover ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Zistcover stores, why it is stored, how it is used, and how users can manage their data.

Zistcover is designed to provide a personalized way for users to organize, save, rate, and manage content within the app.

1. Information We Collect

Zistcover collects and stores only the information necessary to provide its features.

Google Account Information

When you sign in to Zistcover using Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information provided through Google's authentication service, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account/user identifier

This information is used to identify your Zistcover account and associate your personal app data with your account.

Zistcover does not require additional registration information such as your phone number, date of birth, or home address.

Your Zistcover User Data

When you use Zistcover's personalization and organization features, information associated with your account may be stored securely in the cloud.

This may include:

  • Pinned items
  • Loved/favorite items
  • Bookmarked items
  • Completed items
  • Lock or related organization status
  • Custom organization or ordering information
  • Other settings directly related to your use of Zistcover
  • Your ratings and rating history for supported content

This data is associated with your Zistcover account so that your preferences and organization can remain available across supported sessions or devices.

2. Why We Store This Data

Your data is stored to provide and maintain Zistcover's features.

For example, cloud storage allows Zistcover to:

  • Remember your pinned, loved, bookmarked, and completed items.
  • Preserve your organization and personalization settings.
  • Synchronize your supported user data with your account.
  • Remember your ratings.
  • Restore your supported personal app data when you sign in again.

Your personal Zistcover data is not collected for unrelated purposes.

3. Who Can Access Your Personal Data

Your personal Zistcover data is associated with your account.

Zistcover does not sell your personal information.

Your personal organization data, such as your pins, loves, bookmarks, completion status, and related settings, is intended for your account and is not made publicly available to other users.

Ratings that you submit to Zistcover may be used as part of the app's community rating system. Community rating information may be displayed to other users in aggregated form, such as an average rating, rating count, or rating distribution.

4. Cloud Storage and Firebase

Zistcover uses Firebase services provided by Google for account authentication and cloud data storage.

Information transmitted between the app and these services is handled using appropriate security measures, including encryption in transit.

Firebase is used to provide the authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, and rating-related functionality required by Zistcover.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access to account-related data is controlled through authentication and appropriate security rules.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Your account-related data is retained while it is needed to provide Zistcover's features and maintain your account.

Ratings may remain part of Zistcover's community rating information where necessary to maintain the integrity of the community rating system.

If you request deletion of your Zistcover account, the personal data associated with your account will be deleted in accordance with the applicable deletion process, except where retention is required for legitimate legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Zistcover account and associated personal data.

The deletion process should clearly identify that account deletion will remove the personal data associated with your Zistcover account.

If certain information must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes, that information may be retained only for the period required for that purpose.

8. Children's Privacy

Zistcover is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary for the app's functionality and authentication.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Zistcover's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

When significant changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be made available through the app and the published Privacy Policy webpage.
