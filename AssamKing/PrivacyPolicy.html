1. About AssamKing

AssamKing is a free, volunteer-run Android app and website for organising peaceful and lawful public gatherings seeking justice for Zubeen Garg. It lists protests by district, lets people join with one tap, and shows how many people have joined.

The website (paiotorr.com/AssamKing) uses the same account and the same data as the app. Everything in this policy applies to both, and "the app" below means the app and the website.

AssamKing is a fan-run community project. It is not affiliated with Zubeen Garg's family, his official teams, any political party or any government body.

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can delete it. "We" and "us" mean the people who run AssamKing.

The app uses only the internet permission. Organisers choose photos through Android's own photo picker; the app receives only the photo they pick, shrinks it on the phone and uploads that small copy.

The website keeps your sign-in and your chosen district in your browser's own storage, so you stay signed in and your district is remembered. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

3. What we do not collect

  • Your phone number.
  • Your contacts, messages, call logs, files or other photos.
  • Your device location. Map links open in Google Maps or your browser; AssamKing does not track where you go.
  • Advertising IDs. The app contains no advertising or analytics services.

4. How we use information

We use information only to run AssamKing:

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to show protests in your district and the live number of people who joined;
  • to remember which protests you joined and unlock their map links;
  • to let admins and district leaders create and manage protests;
  • to keep the app safe, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell it.

5. Who can see what

  • Other users see protest cards and how many people joined. They never see who joined, and never see your email address or district.
  • The map link of a protest is shown only to people who joined it, and to its organisers.
  • "Created by": the name and role of the admin or district leader who created a protest are shown on that protest to all signed-in users.
  • The people who run AssamKing manage the app's Firebase project and can technically access account records (including email addresses) and which protests an account joined. They use this only to run and protect the app, or when the law requires it.

6. Photos of people on protest cards

Organisers can list people on a protest card (for example an organiser or speaker) with a name, role and photo. Any signed-in user can see them by opening that card, and can tap a photo to see it larger.

These people are also saved to a private list for that district, so organisers can add them again to future protests. Only the main admin and that district's leader can see this list, and either of them can delete an entry at any time.

Organisers must have a person's permission before adding their photo. If you appear on a card and want your photo or name removed, contact us (see Contact us) and we will remove it.

7. Where data is stored

AssamKing stores data with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a service run by Google. Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and Google's servers. Google may process data in India or in other countries where it operates. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your chosen district is also saved on your phone (app) or in your browser (website), so it does not have to be downloaded each time.

8. Sharing with others

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only:

  • with Google, which provides sign-in and data storage for the app (Firebase);
  • when required by law, a court order or a valid request from a government authority;
  • to protect the safety of people or prevent misuse of the app.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account, district and join records: until you delete your account.
  • Protest cards disappear from the app one day after the protest starts. Organisers keep a record of past protests (title, date, district, number of people who joined and who created it) until an admin or the district's leader deletes it.
  • Deleted protests are removed completely, including their join records, people, photos and map link.
  • Saved people in a district's list stay until the main admin or that district's leader deletes them.
  • Google may keep backup copies for a short time under its own policies.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your AssamKing account at any time. Deleting it permanently removes your sign-in account (name, email address and user ID), your joins in every protest (the joined counts go down) and your saved district. Your Google account itself is not affected.

In the app or on the AssamKing website

Tap the AssamKing logo at the top left, then Account, then Delete my account, and confirm. Google may ask you to choose your account once more to confirm it's you. You are then logged out.

On the website

If you no longer have the app, sign in with the same Google account on our delete page and tap Delete my account:

Delete my account

Protests you created as an admin or district leader are not removed by deleting your account. The main admin or the district's leader can delete them.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Change your district: Account in the app or on the website.
  • Leave a protest: tap Leave on its card. Your join record is deleted.
  • Delete your account and data: see Deleting your account.
  • Ask a question, ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or remove something: contact us. We will reply as soon as we can, and within 30 days.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Firebase security rules control who can read and change each piece of data: for example, only you can see your join records and district, only people who joined can see a protest's map link, and only admins and the district's leader can create, edit or delete protests in that district. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and important changes will be shown in the app.
